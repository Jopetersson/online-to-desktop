Privacy Policy

MS Online to Desktop

Last updated: 22 October 2026

MS Online to Desktop is a Safari extension that opens Word, Excel and PowerPoint documents from SharePoint and OneDrive in the Microsoft Office apps on your Mac. It does not collect any data.

What the extension does with data

  • Page addresses. On SharePoint, OneDrive and Microsoft Teams file links, the extension reads the address of the page you are on to recognise when it shows a document.
  • File location. To find the document’s real location, the extension sends one request from your browser to the same SharePoint or OneDrive site you are already signed in to, using your existing sign-in. The response (the file’s path and name) is used only to build an ms-word:, ms-excel: or ms-powerpoint: link.
  • Opening the file. That link is handed to Microsoft Word, Excel or PowerPoint on your Mac. From then on, Microsoft’s own apps and privacy terms apply.
  • Settings. Your choices (automatic or one-press mode, editing or viewing, enabled apps, button position) are stored locally in Safari on your Mac.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not collect, store or send any personal data, documents, browsing history or usage statistics to the developer or to any third party.
  • It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.
  • It does not run on websites other than SharePoint, OneDrive and Teams file links.
