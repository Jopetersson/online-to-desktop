MS Online to Desktop

Open SharePoint and OneDrive documents in Word, Excel and PowerPoint for Mac, automatically or with one press.

Getting started

  1. Open the MS Online to Desktop app once after installing it.
  2. In Safari, choose Safari → Settings → Extensions and turn on MS Online to Desktop.
  3. Allow the extension on sharepoint.com, either in that list or when Safari asks the first time you open a document. Choose Always Allow on This Website.
  4. Click the extension's toolbar button and choose how it should work.

Two ways to open documents

Automatically

Follow a link to a Word, Excel or PowerPoint document and it opens in the desktop app right away. A banner lets you close the browser tab, or the extension can close it for you. Reloading the page keeps the document in Safari.

With one press

The document stays in Safari and an Open in Word (or Excel / PowerPoint) button appears at the bottom right. You can drag it anywhere. The same button is in the toolbar popup.

In the popup you can also choose to open documents for editing or for viewing, and turn the extension on or off for each app.

Requirements

  • macOS 13 or later with Safari
  • Microsoft Word, Excel and/or PowerPoint for Mac
  • A work or school account with SharePoint Online or OneDrive for Business

Troubleshooting

No button appears and nothing opens

Check that the extension is turned on in Safari → Settings → Extensions and allowed on sharepoint.com. Also check that the app it should use is enabled under “Use for” in the toolbar popup.

“Couldn’t find the file’s location”

The extension asks SharePoint where the document is stored, using your existing sign-in. That can fail if you only have anonymous “anyone with the link” access, or for personal (consumer) OneDrive accounts, which are only partly supported. Open the document from SharePoint or OneDrive while signed in and try again.

Word opens but asks me to sign in

Sign in to Word, Excel or PowerPoint with the same work or school account you use in Safari. After that, documents open directly.

Safari asks “Do you want to allow this page to open Microsoft Word?”

That only happens when the extension’s helper isn’t reachable. Open the MS Online to Desktop app once, then try again, or choose Allow.
